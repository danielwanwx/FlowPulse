# FlowPulse adversarial evaluator procedure v1

Evaluate the candidate only at the pre-approval Diagnosis Gate. Require the
initiating change, temporal order, pinned implementation semantics, controlled
flag-off/on contrast, and three distinct direct-parent flag-on payment resolver
failures. Do not require post-repair recovery; that belongs to Verification.

Require Kafka, retry, accounting, or fraud evidence only when the candidate marks that propagation claim proven. Prefer a narrow proven diagnosis over a
broad story. Counter-evidence references must come only from the evidence
supplied with the candidate.
